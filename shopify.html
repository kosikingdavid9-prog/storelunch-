Build Your Online Store with Shopify

Shopify is a platform that gives businesses tools for creating and managing an online store. Here's what beginners should understand before getting started.

What is Shopify?

Shopify is an ecommerce platform that provides tools for creating and managing an online store. It allows businesses to manage products, orders, payments, and other parts of their ecommerce operation from one platform.

What can you do with Shopify?

Shopify provides tools for different parts of running an online store. Depending on the setup and features you use, you can add products, organize your store, manage orders, configure payments, and connect other tools to support your business.

  • Add and organize products and collections.
  • Manage orders and customer information.
  • Customize the appearance of your online store.
  • Connect additional apps and services when needed.

Who is Shopify for?

Shopify can be used by different types of businesses, from small online sellers to established brands. Whether it is suitable for you depends on what you are selling, how you want to operate your store, and the features and costs that fit your business.

What should beginners learn first?

Beginners should first understand how an online store works before focusing on advanced features. Start with the basics of products, store design, payments, orders, customer service, and marketing.

  • Learn how to add and organize products.
  • Understand the basic customer buying journey.
  • Learn how payments and orders are handled.
  • Understand the basics of promoting your store.

Is Shopify right for you?

Shopify may be worth considering if you want a dedicated platform for managing an online store and prefer having many ecommerce tools available in one place. Before choosing it, compare its features, pricing, payment options, and other requirements with your own business needs.
