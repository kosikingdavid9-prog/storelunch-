Shopify for Beginners: A Simple Introduction

Shopify is an ecommerce platform that helps people and businesses create and manage online stores.

What is Shopify?

Shopify is an ecommerce platform that provides tools for creating and managing an online store. Instead of building every part of an ecommerce system from scratch, you can use Shopify to manage products, orders, payments, store design, and other parts of running an online business.

What can you do with Shopify?

Shopify brings several parts of an online store together in one platform. You can use it to create product pages, organize your products, manage orders, customize your storefront, and support customers as they shop online.

Who is Shopify for?

Shopify can be used by beginners, small businesses, and established businesses that want to sell products or services online. The right setup depends on the business model, products, customers, budget, and features the business needs.

What should a beginner learn first?

Start by understanding the basic parts of your store before exploring advanced features. Learn how to add products, organize your storefront, manage orders, understand payments, and provide a clear buying experience for customers.
